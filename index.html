Chronix Video Studio Uploader

Personal short-form video workflow & draft publishing utility

About This Application

Chronix Video Studio Uploader is a private creator desktop utility used to prepare metadata (titles, captions, and hashtags) and upload locally produced short-form documentary videos as drafts to authorized creator accounts via the official TikTok Content Posting API.

All video processing and credential storage occur locally on the creator's workstation. No user data is sold, shared with third parties, or stored on external servers.
