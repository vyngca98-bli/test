Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Information Accessed

When you authorize your TikTok account via TikTok Login Kit, the Application requests only the minimum scopes required to identify your account and upload video drafts (user.info.basic, video.upload, and optionally video.publish).

2. How Data Is Stored & Used

OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored strictly locally on the user's own computer (in a local environment file) and are used solely to transmit video files and captions directly to TikTok's official API endpoints. No personal information, tokens, or video files are collected, transmitted to, or stored on any third-party server operated by the Application.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties. Media files and upload metadata are sent only to TikTok's official APIs upon explicit user action.

4. Revocation & Deletion

You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions, and delete your local .env file on your computer to immediately remove all stored tokens.
